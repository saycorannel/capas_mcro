What We Offer

Our Services

Birth Registration

Registration of live births, delayed registration, and issuance of birth certificates.

Marriage Registration

Processing of marriage licenses, registration of marriages, and issuance of marriage certificates.

Death Registration

Registration of deaths and issuance of death certificates for the municipality.

Certificate Issuance

Certified true copies of birth, marriage, and death certificates from local records.

Legal Documents

Processing of court orders, legitimations, adoptions, and other legal document annotations.

CENOMAR Advisory

Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) requests and civil registration advisory services.

About Us

Serving Capas Since Day One

The Municipal Civil Registrar Office of Capas, Tarlac is the frontline agency responsible for the registration of all civil registry documents in the municipality. We ensure that vital events—births, marriages, and deaths—are properly documented in accordance with Philippine Law.

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of accuracy and honesty in all civil records.

Public Service

Committed to serving every resident of Capas with dignity and respect.

Efficiency

We strive to deliver timely services and minimize processing times.
